ECG features of PVCs

✳️ Broad QRS complex (≥ 120 ms) with abnormal morphologyپ
✳️ Premature — i.e. occurs earlier than would be expected for the next sinus impulse
✳️ Discordant ST segment and T wave changes
✳️ Usually followed by a full compensatory pause
✳️ Retrograde capture of the atria may or may not occur


Frequent or symptomatic PVCs may be due to:

▪️Anxiety
▪️Sympathomimetics
▪️Beta-agonists
▪️Excess caffeine
▪️Hypokalaemia
▪️Hypomagnesaemia
▪️Digoxin toxicity
▪️Myocardial ischemia
