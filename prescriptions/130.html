ECG features:

Sinus tachycardia with a heart rate more than 100 bpm with normal P waves

Atrial tachycardia with a heart rate between 120 to 150 bpm. The P wave morphology may be different from normal sinus rhythm.

Multifocal atrial tachycardia with heart rates from 120 to 200 bpm with 3 or more different P wave morphologiesAtrial flutter with a heart rate of 200 to 300 bpm

Atrial fibrillation with irregularly irregular rhythm and no visible P waves

AVNRT with a heart rate of 150 to 250 bpm, P waves before or within the QRS complex, short PR interval

AVRT with a heart rate between 150 to 250 bpm, narrow QRS complex in orthodromic conduction, and wide QRS complex in antidromic conduction
